# GK Travels CRM

A modern Travel Operations CRM designed for customized tour management, operations tracking, payment monitoring, and travel workflow automation.

## Overview

GK Travels CRM is a centralized operations platform built for travel and tourism businesses to manage:

* Customer leads
* Tour operations
* Trip management
* Payments
* Reminders
* Documents
* Internal task workflows

The system is focused on operational efficiency and improving customer experience for customized travel services.

---

## Features

### Operations Dashboard

* Upcoming departures

* Pending customer balances
* Urgent operational tasks
* Revenue overview

* Centralized Trip Files

* Visa management

* Profit overview

### Document Management
